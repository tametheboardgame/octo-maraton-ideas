# Pun names and possible future marathons

A scratchpad for gloriously questionable October puns. **Nothing here is a confirmed marathon or a formal 2027 contender.** When one of these catches on, create a [GitHub Issue](https://github.com/tametheboardgame/octo-maraton-ideas/issues/new?template=marathon-idea.yml) so it can be discussed and eventually shortlisted. If a better pun turns up, change it.

## Existing ideas, newly christened

- **Floptober**: The biggest box-office bombs. [Idea #1](https://github.com/tametheboardgame/octo-maraton-ideas/issues/1)
- **Rotten-tober**: 21st-century films considered the worst. [Idea #2](https://github.com/tametheboardgame/octo-maraton-ideas/issues/2)
- **Transformtober**: Every Transformers film. [Idea #3](https://github.com/tametheboardgame/octo-maraton-ideas/issues/3)
- **Kurosawtober**: An Akira Kurosawa retrospective. [Idea #4](https://github.com/tametheboardgame/octo-maraton-ideas/issues/4)

## Fresh possibilities for future Octobers

### Directors and performers

- **Carpentober**: Every John Carpenter-directed feature, or a curated horror/sci-fi selection.
- **Spielbergtober**: A Steven Spielberg director retrospective, perhaps by decade.
- **Cagetober**: Nicolas Cage films, from award-winning performances to gloriously wild choices.
- **Coentober**: The Coen brothers' films, watching the mix of crime, comedy and chaos.
- **Arnoldtober**: Arnold Schwarzenegger films, perhaps with an action-film focus.

### Franchises, studios and monsters

- **Godzilltober**: Godzilla films, with an agreed limit on which eras and spin-offs count.
- **Ghiblitober**: Studio Ghibli feature films, including works from multiple directors.
- **Pixartober**: Pixar's feature-film catalogue.
- **Sharktober**: Sharks on screen, from thrillers to outright nonsense.
- **Vamptober**: Vampire films across decades, genres and tones.

### Themes and endurance challenges

- **Schlocktober**: Deliberately cheap, campy or cult B-movies; different from Rotten-tober's critical disasters.
- **Sequel-tober**: Sequels nobody asked for, or unlikely later entries in once-great franchises.
- **Disastober**: Disaster cinema, from spectacular classics to spectacular failures.
- **Monstober**: Classic movie monsters and creature features.
- **Thrilltober**: A month of thrillers, mysteries and twists.

## How to use this list

1. Keep adding half-baked ideas and even worse names here.
2. Promote promising ideas to individual GitHub Issues so links, films and comments can accumulate.
3. When September comes round, include them in the same nomination and shortlist process as the established ideas.
4. Check film counts, running time and UK viewing availability *only when seriously considering an idea*.
