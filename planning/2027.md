**Stage:** Idea collection  
**Target:** Choose the marathon before October 2027.  
**Winner:** Not selected.

## Candidate pool

### 1. Floptober: box-office bomb bonanza

- **Idea:** [#1](https://github.com/tametheboardgame/octo-maraton-ideas/issues/1)
- **Type:** Curated collection.
- **Appeal:** A parade of ambitious failures, famous money-losers and films that might actually be unexpectedly good.
- **Main question:** How do we pick a reasonable number from a huge and changing list?
- **Source:** [Biggest box-office bombs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_biggest_box-office_bombs).
- **Practicality:** Requires selecting a film list and checking availability; reported losses are estimates.
- **Status:** Candidate, not formally shortlisted.

### 2. Rotten-tober: the worst films of the 21st century

- **Idea:** [#2](https://github.com/tametheboardgame/octo-maraton-ideas/issues/2)
- **Type:** Curated collection.
- **Appeal:** Maximum awful-film endurance-test potential.
- **Main question:** Are we after enjoyably awful cinema or the truly unwatchable stuff?
- **Source:** [21st-century films considered the worst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_21st-century_films_considered_the_worst).
- **Practicality:** Requires a carefully chosen selection; 'worst' is subjective.
- **Status:** Candidate, not formally shortlisted.

### 3. Transformtober

- **Idea:** [#3](https://github.com/tametheboardgame/octo-maraton-ideas/issues/3)
- **Type:** Whole franchise.
- **Appeal:** A coherent release-order run through some increasingly absurd robot warfare.
- **Main question:** Are nine films enough for a month? Combine with other giant-robot films or make it a lighter year?
- **Scope:** Include animated theatrical features and spin-offs, with new releases rechecked for October 2027.
- **Practicality:** A defined, manageable filmography, currently nine films rather than a whole month's worth.
- **Status:** Candidate, not formally shortlisted.

### 4. Kurosawtober: Akira Kurosawa retrospective

- **Idea:** [#4](https://github.com/tametheboardgame/octo-maraton-ideas/issues/4)
- **Type:** Director retrospective.
- **Appeal:** A wide-ranging exploration of a landmark filmmaker, from samurai epics to contemporary dramas.
- **Main question:** Complete filmography or curated selection? Some films are very long and early films may be harder to source.
- **Possible anchors:** *Rashomon*, *Ikiru*, *Seven Samurai*, *Yojimbo*, *High and Low*, *Ran*.
- **Practicality:** Check scope, combined runtime and UK viewing availability before committing.
- **Status:** Candidate, not formally shortlisted.

For more possible themes and pun names, browse the [future ideas bank](../ideas/name-bank.md). These are not automatically 2027 candidates; nominate them using Issues if they catch on.

## Shortlisting session — September 2027

1. Open the [idea inbox](https://github.com/tametheboardgame/octo-maraton-ideas/issues?q=is%3Aissue) and include any ideas added since October 2026.
2. Each of us nominates up to three ideas. Pick a shortlist of no more than three.
3. For each finalist, both rate **enthusiasm**, **variety/fun**, and **practicality** from 1 to 5. Max **30 points combined** across two people; the number is a conversation starter, not a binding formula.
4. Confirm the actual film list, UK availability, total running time and viewing schedule before choosing.
5. Record the winner, list and order here. Leave the other ideas in GitHub Issues for future years.
