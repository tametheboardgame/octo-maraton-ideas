# How to use the Octo-Marathon ideas repo

## During the year: spend ten seconds capturing an idea

1. Hit **[Add a marathon idea](https://github.com/tametheboardgame/octo-maraton-ideas/issues/new?template=marathon-idea.yml)**.
2. Put the franchise, theme or challenge in the title.
3. Optionally paste a link or one-line explanation. Submit. That's it. A terrible October pun is encouraged, but optional.

Ideas become GitHub Issues. Comment on an existing idea if you have a better film list, a joke name or a reason it might be unworkable. Avoid duplicate Issues where possible, but capturing the idea is more important than tidiness.

Examples: 'Godzilltober', 'Disastober', 'Cagetober', 'Floptober'. See the [name bank](../ideas/name-bank.md) for more. These are prompts, not commitments.

## In September: turn the inbox into a shortlist

Use [the 2027 planning page](../planning/2027.md) (or copy [the annual template](../planning/annual-template.md) for another year).

- Each person puts forward up to three favourites.
- Reduce to three finalists.
- Each person scores enthusiasm, variety/fun and practicality out of 5 (maximum combined score of 30).
- Check exact scope, runtimes, UK availability and whether the list makes sense for October.
- Agree the winner and record the watchlist. No need to delete the other ideas.

## In October: watch, argue, remember

Keep watch progress on the planning page. Put rankings, discoveries, favourite disasters and memorable moments in that page too.

## Afterwards: keep the tradition recorded

Summarise the finished year in [the archive](../archive/previous-marathons.md). Keep the old planning page and idea Issues, which can still be useful for future marathons.

**Important distinction:** 'Every film' and 'every film in a franchise' need an agreed boundary; theatrical films, animated films, crossovers, spin-offs and television specials can change the count. Make that decision when the idea becomes a finalist.
